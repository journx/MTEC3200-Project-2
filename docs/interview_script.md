"Hi, my name is Journey Ford, and I'm a student working on a design research project. We're currently learning how to use research and data from the real world for our projects, and I'd love to ask you a few questions that will take no more than 10,15 minutes. This is for a class project, and I want to be able to take more accurate notes, so if you'd give me your permission to record without your full name being used, we can make that happen. But if not, that's ok too!"

Interview Questions:

1. How do you find where to go when getting your hair done?
2. Do you feel like there is a major lack of affordable hairstylists in your area who have good reviews?
3. How far do you travel when getting your hair done, and how often are you able to make that trip?
4. When you get your hair done, do you find yourself bothered by any stylist rules and regulations? If so, why?
5. What forms of payment do your hairstylists take? And does this make it easier or harder for you to get your hair done?
6. Does social media make it easier or harder to find a stylist with affordable prices, good quality work, and a location near you?
7. When you get your hair done, do you usually tip? Why or why not?
8. Based on your current experience, does traveling and paying to get your hair done make it easier to continue?
